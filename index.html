Data You Enter Into the App

These apps are designed to work fully offline. Any information you enter — vessel details, calculation figures, report data, or similar — is stored locally on your own device only. We do not operate a server that receives, stores, or has access to this information, and we do not sell or share it with any third party.

Standard Technical Data

Like most Android apps, these apps run within the Android operating system and are distributed through Google Play. As a result, standard technical and diagnostic information (such as device type, operating system version, or crash/performance data) may be collected by the Android platform or Google Play services, governed by Google's own Privacy Policy. This data is not linked to any information you enter within the app itself.

In-App Purchases

For paid apps or in-app purchases, all payment processing is handled entirely by Google Play. We do not receive, process, or store your payment card details, billing address, or other payment information at any point.

No Advertising

These apps do not display third-party advertisements and do not use advertising identifiers for ad targeting.

Data Deletion

Since data you enter is stored locally on your device, you can delete it at any time by clearing the app's data or storage through your device settings, or by uninstalling the app.

Children's Privacy

These apps are professional tools intended for maritime officers and crew, and are not directed at children. We do not knowingly collect information from children.
